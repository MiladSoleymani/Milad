I am Milad Soleymani, a Master’s student in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics at Islamic Azad University (GPA 4.0/4.0), and an AI engineer.

The question that runs through my work is how large models can be adapted — efficiently, and without losing what makes them useful — to settings they were not built for. I work on parameter-efficient fine-tuning of large vision and language models, on reinforcement learning for alignment, on self-supervised and contrastive representation learning for signals that come with few labels, and on evaluation — measuring what a model does rather than what it appears to do. Recently that has meant showing that a model’s architecture, not its prompt, decides whether it can reason in a low-resource language; aligning brain-signal encoders with language so a brain–computer interface can decode commands it was never trained on; and adapting vision transformers to medical imaging.

My research and publications pages go into detail, and my full CV is available to download. I am always glad to hear from researchers working on related problems — the best way to reach me is by email.
