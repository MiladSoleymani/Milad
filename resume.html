Research Experience

  1. Language of Reasoning in Large Language Models: Compliance, Code-Switching, and RL Training Dynamics

    May 2026 — Present · Supervisor: Dr. Nava Eslami

    • Demonstrated that architecture, not prompting, sets whether an 8B model reasons in Persian: on 210 paired MathMist problems a reasoning-channel model complied at 4% against 80–85% for inline-CoT models, and disabling its thinking channel flipped the same weights to 84% (paired McNemar)
    • Showed the chain-of-thought instruction to be near-inert at inference (a 2.9-point language gap) yet decisive under RL, widening to 65–99 points over 300 GRPO steps of a language-blind reward. Also identified a measurement hazard for cross-lingual evaluation: English accuracy falls 91% → 33% as Persian-CoT compliance rises, so a model scoring as robust may never have reasoned in Persian
    • Built the evaluation harness behind both results (vLLM and API inference, sentence-level language ID, paired significance tests, rollout language tracking through GRPO training)

  2. Language-Aligned and Parameter-Efficient Representation Learning for Brain Signals

    Oct 2025 — Present · Supervisor: Dr. Nava Eslami

    • Aligned an ATCNet brain-signal encoder with a frozen sentence-transformer text encoder (CLIP-style contrastive training), decoding motor tasks from natural-language descriptions rather than bare labels, toward zero-shot decoding of unseen commands
    • Adapted that embedding to new subjects with parameter-efficient LoRA (rank 8, ~1% of parameters), targeting the cross-subject variability that limits BCI deployment
    • Reinforcement-learning fine-tuning is underway, with the purpose of reliable cross-subject decoding from minimal calibration data

  3. Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) for Low-Resource Adaptation of Large Vision and Language Models

    Jan 2025 — May 2026 · Supervisors: Dr. Maliheh Sabeti, Dr. Nava Eslami

    • Adapted large pretrained vision transformers (Swin, ViT) to specialist medical tasks by training a small subset of parameters, cutting the compute needed to reach task performance
    • Designed an efficient Swin Transformer for cutaneous melanoma detection, now under review (see publications)

  4. Voice Recognition for Chronic Heart Failure (CHF) Phenotyping

    Aug 2021 — May 2022 · Supervisor: Dr. Andishe Partovi

    • Developed and evaluated models detecting chronic heart failure from voice recordings, with clinicians defining the phenotypes and validating the outputs

  5. Classification and Representation Learning for EEG
